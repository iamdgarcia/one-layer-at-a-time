One Layer at a Time

how a 70B model fits in 4 GB of VRAM, and what each token costs

What you will learn

Layer-streaming inference keeps one layer of the model on the GPU at a time and reads the rest from disk, for every token. VRAM stops being a function of model size: a 70B model fits in 4 GB.

The price is time and disk traffic. Generating one 16-token sentence this way, at 4 GB on an NVMe, reads about 2.2 TB off the disk and takes ~15 minutes. With every layer resident (a 142 GB card), the same sentence takes ~2 s: four hundred times faster, same model.

What to do

Pick a GPU size, a model and a disk. Press Generate and watch the layers travel, token by token, at the speed the hardware dictates. The meters count real disk traffic.

How this works, with sources

The physics model, simple and declared:

time/token = (layers that don't fit) x (layer GB / disk GB/s) + (all layers) x (layer GB / 1000 GB/s GPU ingest)
